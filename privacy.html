Privacy Policy

Unstamp PDF · Last updated October 26, 2026

Unstamp PDF processes your PDF files entirely on your own device. Your files are never uploaded, sent to a server, or shared with anyone.

The app does not collect personal information, use analytics, show ads, or track you.

To run, the app downloads its own program code (two open-source PDF libraries) and fonts from public content networks (cdnjs.cloudflare.com and Google Fonts). Those services receive the standard information any web request includes, such as your IP address. No file contents are ever included.
